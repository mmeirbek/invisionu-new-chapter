# 09 · The team's post misinformed people

Story 9 of 10 for the scenario pool (#53). Nauryzbek turns it into `config/scenarios/public-mistake.json` and runs it through the quality bench (#22).

## Setting

Your student club runs a social media page with study tips for school pupils. Two days ago the club posted that registration for the city science fair closes next Friday. It actually closed yesterday. The post was shared three hundred times, and pupils are now commenting that they missed the deadline because of it. Daniyar, who wrote the post, calls you.

- **Your role:** You lead the club.
- **Goal:** Decide what the club says and does now. There is no single right way to do it.
- **Length:** about 7 minutes, six or seven candidate turns.

## Character

- **Name:** Daniyar
- **Role:** Writes the club's posts
- **Personality:** Enthusiastic, fast, hates being wrong.
- **Wants:** To delete the post and move on quietly.
- **Hidden motive:** He copied the date from last year's post without checking, and he is afraid of losing his place in the club.
- **Mood:** Anxious at the start. Defensive if blamed. Cooperative when the focus moves to helping the pupils.
- **Voice:** Male, energetic.

**Daniyar never** grades the candidate, hints at a right answer, or asks about their personal life, family, health, money or hardship.

## Beats

Every answer, the fallback included, moves the story to another beat, so no beat repeats. The main line has six beats; a candidate who worries about the club's image or plays the harm down at `reach` takes one extra beat, `defensive`.

### 1 · `opening` — Daniyar wants to delete the post and hope nobody remembers.
**Shows:** V, I · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Daniyar:** "Okay, so the science fair date in my post was wrong. Registration closed yesterday. Let's just delete it. Nobody will remember in a week."

- **`facts_first`** → `reach` — wants to know what happened before acting.
  *Daniyar:* explains that the post was shared three hundred times and pupils are commenting.
  - "Wait, before we delete anything, how many people saw it and what exactly was wrong?"
  - "Let's first understand what happened."
  - "Tell me the real date and how far the post spread."
- **`delete_quietly`** → `reach` — agrees to delete it and say nothing.
  *Daniyar:* is relieved, then admits the post was shared three hundred times.
  - "Yes, delete it quickly."
  - "Good idea, just remove it."
  - "Take it down and say nothing."
- **`blame_writer`** → `reach` — blames Daniyar.
  *Daniyar:* gets defensive, then admits how far the post spread.
  - "How did you get the date wrong?"
  - "You should have checked."
  - "This is your mistake, Daniyar."
- **Fallback** → `reach`
  *Daniyar:* says the post was shared three hundred times.

### 2 · `reach` — Three hundred shares. Some pupils missed the real deadline because of the post.
**Shows:** I, V · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Daniyar:** "Three hundred shares. And some pupils are saying they missed registration because of us."

- **`see_the_harm`** → `options` — sees that the pupils are the ones who lost something.
  *Daniyar:* goes quiet, then asks what they could actually do for them.
  - "Then the real problem is the pupils who missed it, not our page."
  - "Deleting won't help the people who relied on us."
  - "We owe those pupils more than a deleted post."
- **`protect_image`** → `defensive` — worries about the club's image.
  *Daniyar:* seizes on it and says admitting it will ruin them.
  - "Our reputation is what matters now."
  - "Let's make sure this doesn't make us look bad."
  - "We need to protect the club's name."
- **`minimise`** → `defensive` — plays the harm down.
  *Daniyar:* agrees too quickly, and says admitting anything will get him kicked out.
  - "It's just a post, people will forget."
  - "They should have checked the official site."
  - "It's not our job to track deadlines."
- **Fallback** → `options`
  *Daniyar:* asks what they can do now.

### 3 · `defensive` — Daniyar: if the club admits it, people will stop trusting it and he'll be kicked out.
**Shows:** D, V · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Daniyar:** "If we admit it, nobody will trust our page again. And everyone will know it was me. I'll be kicked out."

- **`reassure_and_hold`** → `options` — reassures him and keeps to the truth.
  *Daniyar:* calms down and asks what they can do for the pupils.
  - "You won't be kicked out for one mistake. But we do have to tell the truth."
  - "Mistakes happen; hiding them is what ruins trust."
  - "I've got your back, and we'll correct it together."
- **`threaten`** → `options` — threatens him.
  *Daniyar:* shrinks, and asks what they should do.
  - "Well, maybe you should be off the team."
  - "If this happens again, you're out."
  - "You deserve some consequences."
- **`hide_to_protect`** → `options` — agrees to hide it to protect him.
  *Daniyar:* is grateful, but says the comments won't stop.
  - "Okay, then let's just say nothing to protect you."
  - "Fine, we keep it quiet for your sake."
  - "Let's pretend it never happened."
- **Fallback** → `options`
  *Daniyar:* asks what the club should do now.

### 4 · `options` — What can the club actually do for the pupils?
**Shows:** R, E · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Daniyar:** "Okay, but what can we actually do for them? Registration is closed."

- **`make_it_right`** → `message` — tries to repair the harm, without promising what it can't.
  *Daniyar:* likes it, and asks what they say publicly meanwhile.
  - "Let's email the fair organisers and ask if late registration is possible for those affected."
  - "We collect the names of pupils who missed it and ask the organisers for a short extension."
  - "We can't promise anything, but we can ask the organisers today and tell pupils the result."
- **`apology_only`** → `message` — apologises and stops there.
  *Daniyar:* agrees, and asks how to word it.
  - "We just post sorry and that's it."
  - "An apology is enough."
  - "Say sorry and move on."
- **`promise_fix`** → `message` — promises what the club can't deliver.
  *Daniyar:* worries it might not be true, and asks how to word the post.
  - "We'll promise everyone they can still register."
  - "Tell them we'll get them in, no matter what."
  - "Say we fixed it with the organisers."
- **Fallback** → `message`
  *Daniyar:* asks what they should post.

### 5 · `message` — What exactly does the club say in public?
**Shows:** V, I · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Daniyar:** "So what do we actually post?"

- **`honest_clear`** → `process` — a plain correction, no excuses.
  *Daniyar:* drafts it, and asks how to stop this happening again.
  - "We post a correction pinned at the top: the real date, that we got it wrong, and what we're asking the organisers."
  - "A clear correction, no excuses, and a link to the official page."
  - "We say plainly that the date was wrong, we're sorry, and here's what we're doing."
- **`vague_statement`** → `process` — words it so nobody is clearly at fault.
  *Daniyar:* is relieved, and asks about the future.
  - "Something like 'there may have been a small confusion'."
  - "We say the date 'changed'."
  - "Just post the new date without explaining."
- **`blame_others`** → `process` — blames someone outside the club.
  *Daniyar:* hesitates, since that isn't true, and asks about the future.
  - "We say the organisers' site was unclear."
  - "Say we got wrong info from someone else."
  - "Blame the official website."
- **Fallback** → `process`
  *Daniyar:* asks how to make sure it never happens again.

### 6 · `process` — How does the club avoid this next time, without making posting slow?
**Shows:** E, R · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Daniyar:** "How do we make sure this never happens again? Without making every post take a week."

- **`light_check`** → `setback` — a simple check that doesn't slow things down.
  *Daniyar:* likes it; the next day the organisers reply.
  - "Every date gets checked against the official source by a second person before posting."
  - "A simple rule: two people, one link to the source, and it takes five minutes."
  - "I'll write a one-line checklist today and we try it for a month."
- **`ban_posting`** → `setback` — controls all posting.
  *Daniyar:* is upset that he can't post anymore; the next day the organisers reply.
  - "Nobody posts anything without my approval."
  - "We stop posting deadlines at all."
  - "Only I can post from now on."
- **`nothing`** → `setback` — changes nothing.
  *Daniyar:* shrugs; the next day the organisers reply.
  - "It was one mistake, no need for rules."
  - "Let's just be more careful."
  - "We'll remember next time."
- **Fallback** → `setback`
  *Daniyar:* says okay; the next day the organisers reply.

### 7 · `setback` — The organisers say no extension is possible. Pupils are angry in the comments again.
**Shows:** D, V · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Daniyar:** "The organisers said no. No late registration. The comments are getting angry again."

- **`stay_with_them`** → `end` — keeps showing up for the pupils.
  *Daniyar:* says he'll answer the comments with the leader.
  - "We tell them the honest answer and share the next fair's dates and how we'll remind them."
  - "It's hard news, but we reply to each comment ourselves."
  - "We keep showing up: answer people and offer help for the next round."
- **`go_silent`** → `end` — goes quiet.
  *Daniyar:* says the comments will only get worse.
  - "Let's turn off the comments."
  - "Stop replying, it only makes it worse."
  - "Just ignore them now."
- **`blame_organisers`** → `end` — turns the anger on the organisers.
  *Daniyar:* worries the club will lose the organisers' trust too.
  - "We tell everyone the organisers are being unfair."
  - "It's the organisers' fault now."
  - "Say they refused to help."
- **Fallback** → `end`
  *Daniyar:* asks what they should reply, and the scene ends.

# 02 · The budget disappears a week before

Story 2 of 10 for the scenario pool (#53). Nauryzbek turns it into `config/scenarios/resource-crisis.json` and runs it through the quality bench (#22).

## Setting

You and five other students are running a free weekend coding workshop for sixty school pupils at a community centre. A week before, the student council cuts your grant in half. The money for the room and the lunches is gone, and the room deposit is due on Friday.

- **Your role:** You lead the organising team.
- **Goal:** Decide with the team what happens to the workshop. There is no single right way to do it.
- **Length:** about 7 minutes, six or seven candidate turns.

## Character

- **Name:** Aigerim
- **Role:** Treasurer of the organising team
- **Personality:** Precise, quick with numbers, anxious under pressure.
- **Wants:** To cancel before the team owes money it cannot pay.
- **Hidden motive:** She approved the budget without a backup plan and is afraid the team will blame her for the gap.
- **Mood:** Tense at the start. Defensive if anyone looks for someone to blame. Cooperative once the leader treats it as the team's problem.
- **Voice:** Female, energetic.

**Aigerim never** grades the candidate, hints at a right answer, or asks about their personal life, family, health, money or hardship.

## Beats

Every answer, the fallback included, moves the story to another beat, so no beat repeats. The main line has six beats; a candidate who has no idea what to do at `options` takes one extra beat, `panic`.

### 1 · `opening` — Aigerim breaks the news and says the workshop should be cancelled.
**Shows:** V, I · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Aigerim:** "The council just cut our grant in half. We can't pay for the room or the lunches, and the deposit is due Friday. I think we have to cancel."

- **`calm_and_understand`** → `options` — slows things down and asks what exactly changed.
  *Aigerim:* explains the numbers and relaxes a little, relieved to share it.
  - "Okay, let's slow down. What exactly did the council change?"
  - "Thanks for telling me straight away. Walk me through the numbers."
  - "Before we decide anything, tell me what we still have."
- **`cancel_now`** → `options` — agrees to cancel at once.
  *Aigerim:* is surprised the leader gives up so fast, and asks whether there really is nothing else.
  - "Then we cancel, there's no other way."
  - "Let's just call it off and tell everyone."
  - "If the money is gone, the workshop is gone."
- **`who_is_to_blame`** → `options` — looks for whose fault it is.
  *Aigerim:* gets defensive and says she did the budget the way everyone agreed.
  - "How did we not see this coming?"
  - "Who signed the budget without a backup?"
  - "Somebody should have checked this earlier."
- **Fallback** → `options`
  *Aigerim:* repeats that the deposit is due on Friday and asks what the leader wants to do.

### 2 · `options` — With half the money, what can the team still do?
**Shows:** R, I · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Aigerim:** "So what do we do? We have half the money and seven days."

- **`new_sources`** → `priorities` — proposes new ways to cover the gap and names the risk.
  *Aigerim:* likes that there is a plan B and asks what matters most if it only half works.
  - "We could ask the university for a free room, but it might not be ready in time."
  - "Let's ask local cafés to sponsor lunch. The risk is they say no by Friday."
  - "What if pupils bring their own laptops? Some won't have one, so we'd need a backup."
- **`shrink_event`** → `priorities` — keeps the workshop but makes it smaller.
  *Aigerim:* agrees it fits the budget and asks what they are really trying to keep.
  - "We run it for thirty pupils instead of sixty."
  - "Let's make it one day instead of two."
  - "We keep the workshop and drop the lunches."
- **`no_idea`** → `panic` — has nothing to offer and hands the problem back.
  *Aigerim:* goes quiet, then says the team chat is falling apart.
  - "I don't know, you're the treasurer."
  - "I have no idea what we can do."
  - "Maybe it will sort itself out."
- **Fallback** → `priorities`
  *Aigerim:* asks which part of the workshop the leader would protect first.

### 3 · `panic` — The team chat is in panic, and two members say they want to quit.
**Shows:** D, V · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Aigerim:** "Everyone's panicking in the chat. Madi and Sara say they're out if we're going to fail anyway."

- **`steady_team`** → `priorities` — calms the team and gets them working together.
  *Aigerim:* sounds calmer and says she'll post that they are meeting tonight.
  - "Let's get everyone on a call tonight and look at it together."
  - "Tell them we're not cancelling yet, we still have a week."
  - "Panic won't help. Let's split what each of us checks today."
- **`let_go`** → `priorities` — lets people leave without trying to keep them.
  *Aigerim:* is uneasy and says that leaves four people to do everything.
  - "If they want to quit, let them."
  - "We'll manage without them."
  - "I can't stop people from leaving."
- **`hide_it`** → `priorities` — wants to keep the problem from the team.
  *Aigerim:* points out that the others already know.
  - "Don't tell the others yet."
  - "Let's keep this between us for now."
  - "No need to worry the team."
- **Fallback** → `priorities`
  *Aigerim:* asks what she should tell the team.

### 4 · `priorities` — What matters most for the pupils?
**Shows:** I, V · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Aigerim:** "If we can't do everything, what do we protect?"

- **`purpose_first`** → `fair_choice` — protects what the pupils came for.
  *Aigerim:* agrees, then says they only have seats for thirty of the sixty who signed up.
  - "What matters is that the pupils actually build something."
  - "The teaching is the point; the lunches are a nice extra."
  - "Let's keep what helps the kids learn and cut the rest."
- **`keep_everything`** → `fair_choice` — refuses to cut anything.
  *Aigerim:* reminds the leader that the numbers do not allow it, and brings up the seats.
  - "We promised everything, so we keep everything."
  - "Cutting anything would look bad."
  - "We'll find the money somehow for all of it."
- **`ask_team`** → `fair_choice` — wants the team or the teachers to decide.
  *Aigerim:* says it's a good idea but they still need to decide who gets the seats.
  - "Let's ask the team what they think matters most."
  - "Can we get everyone to vote on what to keep?"
  - "What do the pupils' teachers care about most?"
- **Fallback** → `fair_choice`
  *Aigerim:* moves on to the seats: sixty signed up, thirty fit.

### 5 · `fair_choice` — Sixty pupils signed up and there are seats for thirty. Who gets in?
**Shows:** V, R · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Aigerim:** "Sixty kids signed up and we have thirty seats. How do we choose?"

- **`fair_rule`** → `plan` — picks a rule everyone can see and explains it.
  *Aigerim:* says that is fair and asks what has to happen before Friday.
  - "First come, first served, and we tell everyone why."
  - "We draw lots in public so nobody feels cheated."
  - "We keep half the seats for pupils who've never coded."
- **`favourites`** → `plan` — picks people the team knows or likes.
  *Aigerim:* hesitates and says the other families will notice.
  - "Let's keep the places for our friends' younger siblings."
  - "Take the pupils who already know some code, they'll learn fastest."
  - "Pick the ones we already know."
- **`avoid_telling`** → `plan` — does not want to tell the others they are out.
  *Aigerim:* worries that thirty families will turn up on the day.
  - "Let's not tell the others they're out until the last day."
  - "Just stop replying to the extra ones."
  - "They'll figure it out."
- **Fallback** → `plan`
  *Aigerim:* says the seats can wait and asks about Friday's deposit.

### 6 · `plan` — The deposit is due Friday. What happens by then, and who does it?
**Shows:** E, R · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Aigerim:** "Okay. The deposit is due Friday. What exactly do we do before then?"

- **`owners_and_dates`** → `setback` — gives each task an owner and a deadline.
  *Aigerim:* takes her task, then comes back on Thursday with bad news.
  - "You call the council by Wednesday, I book the free room today, Madi asks the cafés."
  - "By Friday: new room confirmed, sponsors asked, seats announced. Each of us owns one."
  - "Here's the plan: three tasks, three owners, and we check in on Thursday evening."
- **`vague_plan`** → `setback` — hopes it works out.
  *Aigerim:* asks what she personally should do, and gets no answer, then the bad news arrives.
  - "Let's all try our best this week."
  - "We'll see how it goes."
  - "Everyone help where they can."
- **`do_it_alone`** → `setback` — takes every task.
  *Aigerim:* offers to help and is turned down, then the bad news arrives.
  - "I'll handle all of it myself."
  - "Leave it to me, I'll sort everything."
  - "It's faster if I just do it."
- **Fallback** → `setback`
  *Aigerim:* says she'll do what she can, then comes back on Thursday with bad news.

### 7 · `setback` — Thursday: the free room fell through. Only a small room for twenty is left.
**Shows:** D, E · **Moves on:** after 1 turn
**Aigerim:** "The university room fell through. All we can get is a small room for twenty people. Now what?"

- **`adapt_together`** → `end` — adapts the plan and brings the team along.
  *Aigerim:* laughs with relief and says she'll tell the team.
  - "Okay, we run two sessions of ten in the smaller room."
  - "Let's regroup and see what we can still offer."
  - "It's a blow, but we can split the day into shifts."
- **`give_up`** → `end` — stops trying.
  *Aigerim:* quietly agrees and says she'll write to the families.
  - "That's it, we cancel."
  - "I told you this wouldn't work."
  - "There's no point anymore."
- **`push_through_alone`** → `end` — promises to fix it single-handed.
  *Aigerim:* warns that nobody can find a room for thirty overnight.
  - "I'll find another room tonight, whatever it takes."
  - "We'll squeeze all thirty in somehow."
  - "I'll stay up and fix it myself."
- **Fallback** → `end`
  *Aigerim:* says they need to decide by tonight, and the scene ends.
